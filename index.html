❓ Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ledgio's security, privacy, and capabilities.

Is Ledgio really 100% free?

Yes, Ledgio is 100% free forever with zero paywalls, trial timers, or hidden premium tiers. As an MIT open-source project, our core mission is providing transparent personal financial tools without monetizing user data.

Where is my data stored, and is it private?

Your ledger is saved on your device first and syncs to your own private account when you sign in. No ads, no third-party trackers, no data selling. We only count anonymous app installs and launches. When cloud sync is enabled, records are transmitted securely and stored in your private Supabase database with strict Row Level Security.

Do I need to connect my real bank account?

No, Ledgio never asks for your bank account credentials, login passwords, or financial aggregator access. You maintain full manual control and privacy over every transaction and category cap you enter.

How do I back up or move my data to a new device?

You can export your entire ledger to portable JSON or CSV spreadsheet files at any time with a single click. When switching devices, simply import your backup file or sign in for secure cloud sync (encrypted in transit, private to your account).

Which devices and platforms does Ledgio support?

Ledgio is built as a progressive web application (PWA) that runs seamlessly across iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux. You can install it directly from your browser to your home screen or desktop for a fast, native offline experience.
