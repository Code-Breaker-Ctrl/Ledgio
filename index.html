Why Ledgio

Most budgeting apps want your bank login. Ledgio doesn't.

Ledgio keeps your entries in your own browser, encrypted with a key only you hold. There is nothing to link, and nothing for us to sell. Syncing between devices is optional.

No bank login

Yours to take

08 • FAQ

Common questions.

Straightforward answers about your data, privacy, and how sync works.

Yes. Ledgio is an open-source project released under the MIT license. There are no paid tiers, no premium paywalls, no trial expirations, and no ads. Every capability—from multi-currency tracking to reports and data export—is completely free.

Your ledger is saved on your device first in browser localStorage. When you sign in, changes sync securely to your private account in Supabase with strict Row Level Security. We never sell your data or show you ads; we only count anonymous app installs and launches.

No. Ledgio intentionally does not connect to bank APIs, require net banking passwords, or integrate third-party screen scrapers. Manual and quick entries give you total deliberate control and privacy over every recorded expense.

You can export your entire ledger to portable JSON or CSV spreadsheet files at any time with a single click. When switching devices, simply import your JSON backup file or sign in to your free account for automatic secure cloud sync (encrypted in transit, private to your account).

Ledgio is built as an installable Progressive Web Application (PWA). It works smoothly across modern browsers on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux. You can add it directly to your home screen or desktop for a fast, full-screen offline experience.
